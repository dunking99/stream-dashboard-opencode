## Design Specificity Verdict

**Half-authored.** The mechanics are genuinely domain-specific: OCR calibration tied to `video.currentTime`, the "−12s behind / closest to live" comparison, league reorder persisted to localStorage, crest fallbacks. Those are real product ideas.

The shell is not. `tailwind.config.js` has an empty `extend`, `App.css` is blank, and `index.css` is stock Tailwind. Strip the copy and this could be a log viewer, crypto ops panel, or CI dashboard. The domain lives in the components; the identity lives nowhere.

**Deterministic scan:** 0 findings, exit 0. The problems are structural and hierarchy-level, not rule-detectable. No browser overlay was possible.

## Overall Impression

Yes — it needs a redesign, but not a repaint. The information architecture is the problem: a failover dashboard where the failover control is fake, the source list is hidden, and configuration is the most prominent thing in the right rail. The Match centre is the one credible, broadcast-like object on screen; it is also the only part with a clear hierarchy.

The single biggest opportunity: make the page answer "what can I watch right now, and which source is closest to live?" above the fold.

## What's Working

1. **"Closest to live / −12s behind"** is the product thesis in one line, not a stats table.
2. **The calibration model** is thoughtfully scoped: freeze frame, read mm:ss, offset, nudges, Auto reset, 45:00 shortcut.
3. **Component-level craft:** crest-to-initial fallback, 2-line name clamp, "Not announced" empty states, `motion-safe` pulse, restrained chevron motion.

## Priority Issues

**[P0] The Auto-Failover switch is decorative.**
It is a green `div` with `cursor-pointer` and no handler. The headline promise is a lie in the UI; clicking it makes the app look broken.
**Fix:** make it a real `role="switch"` wired to backend state, or remove it. Surface the last failover event and its timestamp.

**[P0] First paint and play failures are silent.**
With no data the app points the player at `/proxy/0/...`; `handleSitePlay` failures only reach `console.error`. For an ops tool, that is fatal.
**Fix:** explicit states for backend unreachable, no event selected, and extraction failed, each with a retry and an `aria-live` region.

**[P1] The clock strip is a control panel pretending to be a scoreboard.**
`MAN – – + – – – + AEK` with six unlabelled ± buttons is the most confusing object on screen, and it sits above the only trustworthy score.
**Fix:** read-only score and clock by default. Move ±, Calibrate, and 45:00 into a labelled "Clock" popover with `aria-label`s; never expose the uncalibrated state as clickable dashes.

**[P1] The right rail is configuration-first and triage-last.**
Sites config (which does not drive the backend) is permanently open above the event list. Then come two "Live Events" tabs, 11 sport chips, ~10 league chips, and only then an event. Prime real estate is spent before the first decision.
**Fix:** collapse Sites to one chip row plus a "Manage" sheet. Merge the two event tabs into one list with an optional league grouping. Replace the chip wall with one sport select and only leagues that have playable events.

**[P2] Event cards do not show the only thing the product is about: source availability.**
Each card shows teams, time, and "Finished". No site badges, no playable count, no live dot, no score, no lag. Users must drill four levels to answer "can I watch this?".
**Fix:** one badge row per card: playable site pills, `● Live 63'`, score, and "2 of 3 sources playable". Demote or hide finished events by default.

## Persona Red Flags

**Alex (power user):** no keyboard path (space/`c` to calibrate, `/` to search), no way to compare events by closeness to live, no density toggle. The global single active event prevents side-by-side work. League and event state are not in the URL, so nothing is bookmarkable.

**Sam (accessibility):** body copy is `text-[10px]`/`text-[11px]`/`text-xs`; `text-gray-500` on `gray-800/900` is roughly 3.4–4.0:1 and fails AA; inputs use `outline-none` with border-colour-only focus; ± buttons have `title` but no `aria-label`; score changes are never announced; tabs lack `role="tablist"`/`aria-selected`; status is colour-only.

**Jordan (first-timer):** "Stream Failover Dashboard" names the machinery, not the task. `MAN – – + – – – + AEK` reads as corruption. "Live Events (New - ESPN)" is internal versioning. The first thing asked of him is a homepage URL and a code textarea. Nothing says "pick a match, then pick a source".

## Minor Observations

- Score appears in three places with three states (`--`, `5–1`, none).
- `ESPN · 03:43:15` is an unexplained timestamp.
- The league chips carry counts but no live/upcoming distinction.
- Finished events dominate the default list.
- The left column ends in a large void while match detail is hidden behind tabs.
- The Sites feature is local-only yet visually more important than stream health.
- Native video controls are hidden until hover; channel/quality context is absent.

## Questions to Consider

1. If failover is the product, why is its only control a fake green pill?
2. Delete the clock strip entirely: what is actually lost, and what becomes instantly clearer?
3. Is one active event per process a design decision, or an architecture leak the interface now apologises for?
4. Should the right rail ever contain configuration when the same pixels could rank sources by "closest to live"?
5. Blur all the text: does the layout still read as live sport, or as any monitoring console?
